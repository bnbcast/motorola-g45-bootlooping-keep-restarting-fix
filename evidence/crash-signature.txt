================================================================================
INTERPRETATION
================================================================================
- fault addr 0xffffffffffffff02 == -254. Not a mapped address. Classic poisoned
  pointer from a freed token slot.
- The crash is inside Motorola's oemhook client thread (libmotext_inf.so), which
  calls into the Qualcomm RIL oemhook agent send path.
- The token was already freed/invalidated when the send path tried to convert it.
- qcrilNrd dies -> system_server blocks on Binder -> watchdog reboots after ~60 s.

flapping between LTE/5G they fire every few seconds, which is why the crash
repeated ~14 times per boot.
